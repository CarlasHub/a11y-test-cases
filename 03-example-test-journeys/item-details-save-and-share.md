# Item details, save and share journey

## Goal

Open an item, understand its content, save or remove it and reach the next action.

## Steps

1. Open an item from search results and confirm the page title and main heading identify the item.
2. Navigate the content by headings, landmarks and links.
3. Confirm lists, metadata and any tables preserve their relationships and reading order.
4. Save and remove the item with keyboard and pointer input.
5. Confirm the saved state and status message are visible and programmatically exposed.
6. Operate share controls and dismiss any popover or dialog.
7. Activate the next action and record the destination, new-window behaviour and any external-platform handoff.
8. Repeat at 200 percent text size and the equivalent of a 320 CSS-pixel viewport.

## Required states

- item available and, if testable, unavailable or expired;
- saved and unsaved;
- share controls closed and open;
- sticky action control at different scroll positions;
- internal and external destinations.

## Related tests

Run TC-01 through TC-07 and TC-09 through TC-13.
