# Registration and subscription journey

## Goal

Submit a registration or subscription form after identifying and correcting errors.

## Steps

1. Review the instructions, privacy information, required fields and expected formats before entering data.
2. Submit the form empty and record each error's text, association, focus behaviour and announcement.
3. Enter malformed values and confirm known correction suggestions are provided.
4. Complete autocomplete, select, checkbox and custom controls with keyboard only.
5. Confirm visible labels are contained in accessible names and qualifying user-information fields expose the correct input purpose.
6. Submit safe fictional data and confirm success is visible and announced.
7. If the process has multiple steps, confirm data is preserved and not requested repeatedly without a valid reason.
8. Review any confirmation, unsubscribe or profile-management process within the authorised scope.
